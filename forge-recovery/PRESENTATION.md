# FORGE — five-minute live demo

## Before the slot

Open the app, verify Claude is connected, then use the top-right profile menu to reset the incident. Keep the manager view selected. Close any editor containing .env. Decide who speaks and who drives. If the hosted page requires owner sign-in, present from the signed-in laptop or run locally; the public source repo is independently accessible.

## 0:00–0:30 — the customer problem

Say: “Factories do not need another dashboard telling them a machine is down. They need to know what customer promise breaks next — and what to do now. FORGE helps the plant manager make that recovery decision across planning, quality, maintenance and materials.”

Point at the six affected commitments and USD 4.042M of synthetic order-line value. Say this is a source-data replay of a constrained leak-test resource. Do not describe the source dates as shipments due today.

## 0:30–3:30 — the live workflow

1. Point to the left order book: all 60 commitment lines, backed by source records. Select COM-0055 to show its customer, configuration and approved promise, then close its detail.
2. Click **Find a recovery path**. Explain that Claude interprets the source evidence and prioritizes the affected commitments; application code checks the hard gates.
3. Preview A and B. A is resequencing plus overtime; B is expedite plus qualified substitution. The source supplies their costs and aggregate impact.
4. Preview C. Explain: “There are 111 units on hand, but zero eligible. Held stock cannot become usable because the model recommends it.” Show the evidence, then return to A.
5. In the right-hand chat, ask **Why choose A over B?** Select COM-0055 and **Discuss this order** for a customer-specific follow-up if time allows. Show the source citations.
6. Click **Review & approve**, inspect the prerequisites, and approve A. Explain: “This approves coordination. It does not bypass reconciliation, staffing or Quality, and it does not dispatch equipment.”
7. Open **Execution**. Use the top-right profile menu to select the supervisor presenter view. Acknowledge one task. Point out the owner and concrete action.

## 3:30–4:30 — the business case

Say: “The buyer owns on-time delivery and disruption costs: the VP of Operations or plant manager. FORGE is designed to shorten the investigation and coordination required for a recovery decision.”

Show CAD 18,400 for A versus CAD 26,750 for B. Explain these are synthetic source assessments, separate from USD order value. Do not claim USD 4.042M in savings, protected revenue, or measured time savings.

## 4:30–5:00 — the AI distinction and close

Say: “Claude explains evidence across systems, prioritizes the affected commitments and answers business questions in context. Deterministic code owns the constraints and source economics; the manager owns approval. This demo compares bounded source strategies, and exposes the facts still needed before dispatch.”

Close: “FORGE turns a factory disruption into a recovery decision the team can understand and act on.”

## If connectivity fails

Use the clearly labeled source-alternatives fallback to show validation and the approval workflow. Say that this mode makes no AI analysis call; do not present it as live Claude. Keep a short screen recording made from the actual app as an optional backup.
