# FORGE: recover the shipment when the test station fails

## One sentence

FORGE is an AI recovery agent that helps liquid-cooling manufacturers protect customer shipments when a shared test station fails.

## The 30-second pitch

When a shared thermal, flow, or leak-test station goes down, production teams must quickly decide what to test first, which qualified capacity remains, and which customer promises they can still protect. That decision spans production, quality, maintenance, and shipping.

FORGE traces the affected orders, calculates shipment exposure, and compares feasible recovery plans. The plant manager chooses the trade-off; the supervisor gets a specific execution sequence.

## The demo: one incident, one decision

1. **Trigger:** simulate an eight-hour T-02 thermal-test outage.
2. **Consequence:** fourteen orders share the queue. Three shipment commitments miss without recovery; $1.4M is exposed. T-01 has six regular hours left.
3. **Choice:** A protects the $900K priority shipment; B protects all three with $7,800 overtime; C uses a qualified internal validation cell at a higher setup/staffing cost.
4. **Proof:** inspect exact test windows, customer deadlines, station qualifications, and cost assumptions. Change the outage duration and recalculate.
5. **Decision:** route Plan B; record the plant manager's name and rationale.
6. **Handoff:** the supervisor accepts order-specific readiness, testing, quality, and shipping tasks. Every simulated completion is recorded.

The amounts are synthetic scenario calculations, not validated customer savings. The default recommended plan has $11,800 total estimated incident cost, including $7,800 overtime and $4,000 failed-station idle exposure. Shipment value protected is not added to cost savings.

## Who buys

VP Manufacturing, Plant Manager, or VP Operations. Validate willingness to pay against a specific plant's incident frequency, manual coordination time, deadline misses, expedite spend, and overtime authorization process.

## What to leave out

No multi-company tour, inter-site transfer, generic operations-platform claim, general-purpose chatbot showcase, or autonomous factory-control claim. No “every liquid-cooling unit is always tested” market claim without customer-specific evidence.

## The claim to test

Can FORGE produce a qualified, reviewable recovery plan in under 60 seconds, and shorten the plant's observed time to an approved response?

Show calculation latency and the human approval cycle separately. Do not claim a measured improvement over human teams until a representative manual baseline exists.
